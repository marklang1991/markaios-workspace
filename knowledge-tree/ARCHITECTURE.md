# Knowledge Tree Architecture

## Layers

```text
Mark Knowledge Tree
├── 00 Navigation and governance
├── 01–11 Long-lived knowledge domains
├── 12 Project Hub
├── 98 Inbox
└── 99 Archive
```

### 00 Navigation and governance

Defines naming, page placement, maintenance cadence, and sync policy.

### 01–11 Long-lived domains

Each domain should gradually converge on the same internal shape:

```text
00 Overview
10 Methods
20 Project records
30 References
90 Decision log
99 Archive
```

The current domains are:

1. Work and career
2. Quantitative investing
3. Personal AI system
4. Motorcycles
5. Podcasting
6. Camping
7. Family
8. Games and culture
9. Immigration and legal
10. Life and consumption
11. Tools and skills

### 12 Project Hub

Projects are temporary containers with one outcome, explicit success criteria, milestones, next actions, risks, decisions, and a retrospective. When a project finishes, reusable knowledge moves to a domain and process material moves to the archive.

### 98 Inbox

The inbox accepts rough notes, malformed titles, imports, and uncategorized material. It should be reviewed weekly.

### 99 Archive

The archive preserves history without polluting active navigation. Nothing should be archived until its reusable conclusions have been extracted.

## Naming conventions

- Domain: `NN emoji Topic`
- Project: `YYYY-QN | Project name | Status`
- Reference: `Topic | Source | YYYY-MM-DD`
- Decision: `DEC-YYYYMMDD | Decision topic`
- Retrospective: `RETRO | Project name | YYYY-MM-DD`

## Weekly maintenance

- Classify or discard inbox items.
- Update active project next actions and blockers.
- Distill reusable conclusions into domain pages.
- Archive completed or superseded material.
- Update the GitHub map when the top-level structure changes.

## Privacy boundary

Do not sync contact details, identity documents, immigration records, family details, financial positions, trading rules, contracts, credentials, or confidential employer/project information to GitHub. Notion remains the private system of record for those materials.
